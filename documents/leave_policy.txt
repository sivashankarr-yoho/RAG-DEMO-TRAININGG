YOHO TECHNOLOGIES - EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

1. Casual Leave

Employees receive 12 casual leaves per year.

Casual leaves are intended for personal work
and emergency situations.

2. Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 sick leaves per year.

Employees should inform their reporting manager
when taking sick leave.

3. Leave Approval

Employees must apply for leave through the HRMS portal.

Manager approval is mandatory before taking leave.

4. Leave Carry Forward

Unused casual leaves cannot be carried forward
to the next calendar year.
